How it works

Three steps to smarter shopping.

01

Scan

Point your camera at any barcode or QR code. Product nutrition data loads instantly from Open Food Facts.

02

Price

Enter the price you see on the shelf. The app calculates price per gram of protein, protein per dollar, and a value rating.

03

Compare

Save your scans, browse community data, and find the best protein value at any store near you.

Features

Stop overpaying for protein.

📷

Barcode & QR scanning

Supports standard barcodes and QR codes from store apps and websites. Scans resolve in under a second.

📊

Three calculation modes

Enter quantity by servings, weight, or volume. Auto-detects the best mode for each product. Handles unit conversions automatically.

🌍

Community pricing data

Every scan you save contributes to a shared database. Browse what others are paying for the same products at different stores.

🔒

Privacy-first

Works fully offline. No account required. Your notes stay private. You control what you share.

Open & community-driven

Better data from real shoppers.

Protein Pricer uses the Open Food Facts database — a free, open, collaborative food database. Pricing data is crowd-sourced from users like you. The more people scan, the more useful it gets for everyone.
