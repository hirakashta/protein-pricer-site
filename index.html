How it works

Three steps to smarter shopping.

01

Scan

Point your camera at any barcode, or pick from 76+ fresh proteins like chicken, fish, eggs, and more. Product nutrition data loads instantly.

02

Price

Enter the price you see on the shelf. The app calculates price per gram of protein, protein per dollar, and a value rating.

03

Compare

Save your scans, browse community data, and find the best protein value across products and stores.

Features

Stop overpaying for protein.

📷

Barcode scanning & fresh protein picker

Scan any product barcode, or choose from 76+ fresh proteins across 9 categories. Results load in under a second.

📊

Three calculation modes

Enter quantity by servings, weight, or volume. Auto-detects the best mode for each product. Handles unit conversions automatically.

🌍

Community pricing data

Save your scans to a shared price database. Browse what others are paying for the same products at different stores.

🔒

Privacy-first

No account needed to scan and calculate. Contributions are anonymous by default. Your notes are never shared. Full account deletion with data anonymization. No ads, no trackers.

Every scan gets a clear rating so you can decide at a glance.

Open & community-driven

Better data from real shoppers.

Protein Pricer uses the Open Food Facts database — a free, open, collaborative food database. Pricing data is crowd-sourced from users like you. The more people scan, the more useful it gets for everyone.
